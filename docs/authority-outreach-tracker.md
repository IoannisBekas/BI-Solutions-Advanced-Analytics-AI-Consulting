# Authority outreach tracker

Use this document for real relationships and firsthand contributions only. The
goal is a useful third-party reference that readers would value even if search
engines did not exist—not a purchased or reciprocal backlink.

## Evidence required before outreach

For every target, record:

- the real person or organization and the existing relationship;
- the project, event, article, tool, or expertise that makes the outreach
  relevant;
- whether the client or partner permits its name and engagement to be
  mentioned;
- the exact destination page or contribution format;
- the owner who will send or approve the message.

Do not claim a client relationship, result, certification, partnership, or
endorsement without written permission and supporting evidence.

## Review before any message is sent

- The recipient and relationship are real.
- The message contains no confidential details.
- Named organizations and results have written publication permission.
- The requested page or contribution is relevant to its audience.
- No payment, reciprocal link, incentive, or required positive wording is
  offered.
- The final message and destination have been approved by Ioannis.
