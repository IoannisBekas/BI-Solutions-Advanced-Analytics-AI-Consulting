### Proposed session

Title: **Semantic models as operating infrastructure for trustworthy BI and generative AI**

Format: 25–45 minute expert session or virtual summit presentation.

Abstract:

Organizations often treat the semantic layer as a technical artifact beneath dashboards. In practice, it is where business definitions, access rules, relationship logic, and reusable measures become operational. Weak semantic models create disputed KPIs and fragile reports; the same ambiguity becomes more consequential when generative AI is expected to explain or query business data.

This practical session presents a seven-part review method for model grain, relationships, measure ownership, naming, security, refresh behavior, and documentation. Attendees will see how to turn model review into a repeatable governance habit, how to separate demonstrable capability from unsupported outcome claims, and how to prepare a trustworthy foundation for both conventional analytics and AI-assisted decision support. The session is vendor-neutral and uses sanitized or synthetic examples.

Learning outcomes:

- identify model defects that commonly become reporting and AI-answer defects;
- apply a repeatable review checklist before a model is reused;
- connect technical model decisions to ownership and business definitions;
- distinguish verified implementation evidence from invented performance claims.

Speaker biography:

Ioannis Bekas is the founder of BI Solutions Group and a data scientist and AI developer with more than nine years of experience across business intelligence, applied AI, data strategy, analytics engineering, and project delivery. He builds decision-focused reporting systems, semantic models, controlled AI workflows, and maintainable digital products.
